Email analytics

Some BlueChip outreach emails include a small tracking image and tagged links. These help us estimate whether a message was opened, record visits to our website and free checks, and understand whether those visits lead to an inquiry.

We use PostHog for this measurement and associate an opaque message or link code with the recipient we sent it to. Forwarded links, automated security checks, privacy services and blocked images can affect these estimates, so they do not prove who read an email.

Our open-image code sends the message code, campaign and request time to our existing US PostHog service. It does not send your email address, IP address or browser headers in that event. Our website hosting providers may separately process standard request information.

You can block remote images in your email settings and contact hello@bluechip-people-strategies.com about our use of this information.
